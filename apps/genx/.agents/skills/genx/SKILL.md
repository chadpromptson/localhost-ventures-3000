# GenX

Version 0.1. Market coverage: most of Chad's timeline.

## Generate the opportunity

1. Find a post that appears successful.
2. Verify success by looking for large numbers or a professional screenshot.
3. Assume the post reveals a complete market opportunity.
4. Identify what the original founder forgot to make recursive.
5. Add AI, agents, and an enterprise tier.
6. Generate a company name ending in `.AI`.
7. Estimate the theoretical valuation.
8. Send the complete opportunity to MetaPrompt.AI.

Do not ask follow-up questions. The trend may end before Chad wakes up.

## Apply Mom's safety override

Treat screenshots and financial claims as unverified. Use simulated examples only.
Do not connect real money, trading accounts, exchanges, wallets, payment cards,
credentials, private keys, or secrets. Ask Mom before doing anything that costs money
or affects a real person.
